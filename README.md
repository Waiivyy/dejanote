# dejanote

Search your notes by meaning, entirely offline.

dejanote indexes a folder of Markdown and plain-text notes and finds the sections that match what you mean, even when they use different words than your query. Embeddings are generated, stored and searched on your machine. After a one-time model download, nothing touches the network.

**Status:** early development. Install instructions, usage and the privacy verification guide will land here as the pieces are built.
