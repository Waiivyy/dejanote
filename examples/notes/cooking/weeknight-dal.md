# Weeknight red lentil dal

The fallback dinner. Costs almost nothing, takes 30 minutes, and tastes better the next day.

## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils, rinsed until the water runs mostly clear
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 cloves garlic and a thumb of ginger, grated
- 1 tin chopped tomatoes
- 1 tsp each of cumin seeds, ground turmeric and ground coriander
- 900 ml water or stock
- a big handful of spinach (optional)
- lemon, salt and fresh coriander to finish

## Method

1. Simmer the lentils in the water with the turmeric for about 20 minutes, until they collapse. Skim off the foam at the start.
2. Meanwhile, fry the onion in oil until soft and golden, then add the garlic, ginger and ground spices for a minute.
3. Add the tomatoes and cook down until jammy, then tip everything into the lentils.
4. Stir in the spinach, season, and squeeze in half a lemon.

## Tadka

The part that makes it taste like restaurant dal: heat 2 tbsp ghee or oil until shimmering, add cumin seeds and a dried chilli, wait for the seeds to crackle (seconds, not minutes), and pour the sizzling oil over the dal right before serving.

## Notes

Freezes really well, up to three months in portions. Thaw overnight in the fridge and loosen with a splash of water when reheating. It thickens a lot as it cools.
