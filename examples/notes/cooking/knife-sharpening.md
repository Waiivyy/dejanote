# Knife sharpening

Finally learned to do this properly instead of using the pull-through gadget, which was chewing up the edge.

## Honing is not sharpening

The steel rod only straightens the edge that is already there. Do it every few uses. Once it stops helping, the knife needs real sharpening on a stone.

## Whetstone routine

- soak the stone for 10 minutes (mine is a soaking stone, splash-and-go ones do not need it)
- 1000 grit for the main work, 4000 or 6000 to polish
- hold the angle: around 15 degrees for the Japanese gyuto, 20 for the German chef's knife
- raise a burr along the whole length on one side before switching sides
- finish with light alternating strokes, then strop on newspaper or leather

The marker trick helps: colour the edge with a permanent marker and check where the ink gets removed. If only the shoulder is clean, the angle is too low.

## Test

A sharp knife slices a tomato with no pressure and catches on a thumbnail instead of sliding off.
