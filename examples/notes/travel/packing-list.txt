PACKING LIST (carry-on only)

Documents: passport, ID card, insurance card, printed booking confirmations, a little local cash.

Electronics: phone and charger, travel adapter, power bank (in hand luggage, never checked), headphones, e-reader.

Clothes for a week: three tops, two trousers, one warm layer, rain jacket, underwear and socks for five days (wash on day four), comfortable walking shoes worn on the plane.

Toiletries in a clear bag, liquids under 100 ml each: toothbrush, toothpaste, deodorant, sunscreen, contact lens solution, plasters, ibuprofen, any prescriptions in their original packaging.

Do not forget: reusable water bottle (empty through security), earplugs and an eye mask, a pen for landing cards.
