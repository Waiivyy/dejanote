# Tomatoes on the balcony

Six plants this year: two cherry varieties, a beefsteak and three plum tomatoes, in 30 litre pots.

## Watering and feeding

Water deeply and regularly, every day in a heatwave. Irregular watering is what caused the black, sunken patches on the bottom of the fruit last year (blossom end rot): the plant cannot move calcium into the fruit when the soil swings between bone dry and soaked. Mulching the pots helped keep the moisture even.

Once the first flowers set fruit, feed weekly with a high potash tomato feed.

## Side shoots

For the cordon varieties, pinch out the shoots that grow in the angle between the stem and a leaf while they are still small. Leave the bush cherry tomato alone. Stop the main stem once it reaches the top of the cane, usually in early August.

## Problems

- yellowing lower leaves late in the season are normal, just remove them
- blight shows up as brown patches that spread fast in wet weather; remove affected leaves immediately and keep the foliage dry
- split fruit after a rainstorm comes from sudden heavy watering after a dry spell
