# Moving flat checklist

Used this for the move in August, keeping it for next time.

## Four weeks before

- give notice on the old place in writing and confirm the handover date
- book the van and ask two friends who have their own boxes
- start eating through the freezer

## The week before

- take photos of every room in the old flat for the deposit
- photograph the meter readings on moving day, in both flats
- redirect post for six months
- tell the bank, the employer, the doctor and the insurance about the new address
- the broadband contract takes two weeks to move, so do it early

## After

- register the new address with the city within two weeks
- update the address on shopping accounts so parcels stop going to the old place
- send the old landlord the forwarding address for the deposit
