# Boiler pressure

The combi boiler loses pressure every couple of months and the heating and hot water just stop. The display shows fault F22 when it drops too low.

## Normal range

The gauge should read between 1 and 1.5 bar when the system is cold. Up to about 2 bar while the heating is running is fine. Below 0.5 it locks out.

## Topping it up (filling loop)

1. Turn the boiler off and let the radiators cool down.
2. Find the braided silver hose under the boiler, with a tap at each end.
3. Open both taps slowly and watch the gauge climb. Close them at 1.2 bar.
4. Turn the boiler back on and press reset if it is still showing the fault.
5. Check for drips around the hose afterwards.

## If it keeps dropping

More than once a month means something is wrong: a slow leak somewhere (check the radiator valves and the floor near the pipes), the pressure relief valve letting water out through the pipe outside, or an expansion vessel that needs re-charging. That is a job for the engineer, not me. Last service was in October.

## Bleeding radiators

If the top of a radiator stays cold, there is air in it. The radiator key is in the drawer by the sink. Bleed with the heating off, then top the pressure back up, because it drops afterwards.
