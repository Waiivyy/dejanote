# The Mom Test, by Rob Fitzpatrick

A short book about talking to customers without them lying to you to be nice.

## Main rule

Ask about their life and what they have actually done in the past, not about your idea and what they might do in the future. Opinions and hypotheticals are worthless; past behaviour and money already spent are signal.

- bad: "Would you use an app that does X?"
- good: "When did that last happen? What did you do about it? What did it cost you?"

## Compliments are noise

If someone says "great idea!", you have learned nothing. Deflect it and steer back to specifics.

## Commitment

A meeting only went well if it ended with a commitment: time, reputation or money. Otherwise it was just polite.

## Use it for

The meal planning app interviews next month. Write three questions about how people decide what to cook today, and nothing about features.
