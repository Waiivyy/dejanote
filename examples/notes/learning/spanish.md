# Spanish notes

## Ser vs estar

Both mean "to be". Ser is for what something is: identity, origin, profession, time, what it is made of. Estar is for states and locations: how you feel, where something is, the result of a change.

- Soy de Múnich. (origin)
- Estoy cansado. (temporary state)
- La fiesta es en mi casa. (events use ser even for location, the classic trap)
- Está muerto. (death uses estar, which feels wrong but is right)

## Preterite vs imperfect

Preterite for completed actions with a clear end: ayer comí paella. Imperfect for background, habits and descriptions in the past: cuando era niño, comía paella cada domingo.

## Subjunctive triggers

Wishes, emotions, doubt and recommendations about someone else: quiero que vengas, me alegra que estés aquí. After "cuando" for things that have not happened yet: cuando llegues, llámame.

## Vocabulary that keeps slipping

- aprovechar: to make the most of
- madrugar: to get up very early
- echar de menos: to miss someone
