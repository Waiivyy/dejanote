# Questions to ask when interviewing

The interview goes both ways. Things I want to know before accepting an offer.

## About the team

- How long have people on the team been here? What happened to the last person in this role?
- What does a typical week look like, and how much of it is meetings?
- How are decisions made when engineers disagree?

## About the work

- What is the on-call rotation like, and how often does it actually page at night?
- What did the team ship recently that you are proud of?
- What is the biggest piece of technical debt, and is there time to pay it down?

## About growth

- How do promotions work, and what did the last person who got promoted do to get there?
- Is there a budget for conferences or courses?

Red flags from past interviews: nobody could describe how success is measured, and one hiring manager badmouthed the previous engineer.
