# Sleep

Things that noticeably helped, from three months of writing down how I feel in the morning.

- same wake-up time every day, weekends included, even after a late night
- no coffee after 2pm (an afternoon espresso still keeps me awake at bedtime)
- bedroom cool, around 18 degrees, and properly dark
- the phone charges in the kitchen, not next to the bed
- if I am still awake after 20 minutes, get up and read on the sofa until sleepy instead of lying there

Did not help: the sleep tracking app itself, which made me anxious about the scores.

Alcohol makes me fall asleep faster but I wake up at 3am, so it is not worth it on work nights.
