# Running: 10k plan

Goal: 10k under an hour by September. Currently running 5k in 31 minutes.

## Weekly structure

- Tuesday: easy run, 5 to 6 km at a pace where I could hold a conversation
- Thursday: intervals, 6 x 400 m fast with 90 seconds of walking between
- Sunday: long slow run, adding about 10% distance each week, and every fourth week drop back

Rest days are part of the plan, not a failure of it.

## Shin splints (again)

The ache along the inside of the shin came back in week three, same as last spring. What helped last time:

- cut the weekly distance by a third for two weeks and swap one run for cycling
- calf raises and toe walks every evening
- ice for ten minutes after runs
- new shoes: the old pair had about 900 km on them, and they should be replaced every 600 to 800 km

If it hurts to walk, or one spot is very tender to touch, see a physio instead of running through it.
