# Budget

Rough monthly split of take-home pay, loosely the 50/30/20 idea:

- 50% needs: rent, utilities, groceries, insurance, transport
- 30% wants: eating out, hobbies, travel fund
- 20% saving and investing, moved automatically on payday so it never feels like spare money

## Emergency fund

Target: three months of essential expenses in an instant-access savings account, separate from the current account so it is not tempting. Reached it in March. Only for genuine emergencies: losing the job, a broken boiler, vet bills.

## Subscriptions audit

Went through the bank statements in January and cancelled the second streaming service, an app I had forgotten about, and the gym I had not visited since October. Saves about 45 a month.
