# Tax return checklist

What to collect every spring so the return takes an afternoon instead of a week.

## Documents

- annual payroll statement from the employer
- bank statements showing interest earned, plus the capital gains summary from the broker
- receipts for work equipment (laptop, desk chair, monitor) and the count of home office days
- charity donation receipts
- health insurance and pension contribution statements
- commuting days and distance for the travel allowance

## Process

Use last year's return as a template, it saves an hour. Upload receipts as PDFs into the year's folder as they come in, not all at once in May.

## Deadlines

Without an adviser the deadline is in the summer; with an adviser it is much later. Check the current year's date, it has moved before.
